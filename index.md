# SparPilot

**Last reviewed: 24 October 2026**

SparPilot is a privately operated, AI-assisted personal finance project. **Live, read-only account-information connections are currently active for Nordea and SAS EuroBonus Mastercard.** Banking access is subject to the user's separate authorizations and the limitations of each provider.

SparPilot can show account and card information, balances and transactions and help interpret spending and potential savings. The connected ChatGPT plugin does **not** provide payment initiation or transfers. It can save user-defined account display names and manage banking authorization states, so "read-only" describes financial transactions, not every technical operation.
