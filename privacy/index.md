# SparPilot — Privacy Policy
**Last updated: 18 October 2026**

SparPilot is a personal, read-only financial analysis project operated by its individual owner. It is currently under development. The production connection to Enable Banking and Nordea is **not yet enabled**. This notice describes the intended production processing; actual features are limited to those deployed at any given time.

## Controller and contact
The individual operating SparPilot is responsible for decisions about personal financial data processed by their private installation. For privacy enquiries, use the contact email recorded for the SparPilot application in Enable Banking's application listing. This public page intentionally does not publish banking credentials, account identifiers or private contact information.

## Data and purpose
If a production connection is separately activated, SparPilot is intended to access only accounts expressly authorized through the bank's and Enable Banking's account-information consent process, for transaction review, budgeting, reconciliation and potential savings suggestions. Depending on available access, the data may include account identifiers, balances, dates, amounts, counterparties and transaction descriptions. Access to accounts held under a power of attorney or disponent role is not presumed.

The application is intended to request account-information (AIS) access only. It is not intended to initiate payments.

## Storage and access
The proposed architecture processes financial data on the user's own computer and uses local encryption for saved imports. Synthetic test data are currently stored locally in encrypted form. The planned production storage, credential management, logging and deletion controls must be independently reviewed and tested before real financial data are imported. We do not claim that these controls are already deployed for live banking.

Enable Banking and the relevant bank process authentication and authorization under their own terms and privacy notices. No third-party advertising or sale of financial data is intended.

## Legal basis, consent and withdrawal
Bank account access will be initiated only after the user completes the relevant bank/provider authorization. A bank's PSD2 authorization is distinct from any legal basis for subsequent personal-data processing. For a purely personal or household activity, applicable data-protection rules may depend on the circumstances. Any broader use would require a separate assessment.

The user can withdraw bank access through the relevant bank/provider mechanisms when available. Local removal of saved data does not by itself revoke a remote bank authorization. Revocation and deletion procedures will be confirmed before production activation.

## Retention and rights
Synthetic development data may be retained locally for testing and removed by the operator. No fixed production retention period is asserted because production processing is not operational; the retention and deletion policy must be finalized before activation. For access, correction or deletion of local information, contact the operator using the application contact details.

## Updates
This notice will be revised before production launch and when data handling materially changes.
